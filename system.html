How they're connected

  EDITOR ──write──▸ DRAFT
                      │
                   publish  ──▸ menu_versions v47   (immutable)
                      │              │
                      │         live_version = 47
                      ▼              │
                    TABLE ◂──read────┘   caches by version, never sees a draft
                      │
                   settle  ──▸ settlements[]  ──▸ DASHBOARD
                                                    tonight's statement
                                                    tips owed by server
1 Publishing mints an immutable version. A diner's menu can never change under their thumb mid-meal, and rolling back is just pointing live_version at the one before.
2 An open check is priced at order time. Reprice the menu +8% and tonight's bills do not move.
3 Settlement only moves forward on a provider callback or an operator action. A diner returning to the page is not confirmation.
4 Cash declared is a claim, not a collection. It stays pending until staff confirm they have it, and pending cash is left out of confirmed totals.
5 Translations are keyed by dish id, never by position. A missing translation is marked as missing; the editor never passes English off as French or Arabic.
This browser stands in for the backend. The pages share one origin, the same shape they'd have as routes on a real server. Open the editor and the table in two tabs, publish, and watch the menu change. Payments are simulated: no money moves and no provider is connected.
