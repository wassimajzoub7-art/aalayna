Customer relationships & value

Know who comes back.
Know what to do next.

The ambition is to answer an owner's practical questions: Who are my valuable regulars? Who has stopped visiting? Which invitation deserves my budget? What happened afterward?

From a receipt to a return visit

  1. Connect with permission. Offer a receipt by email or WhatsApp, with a separate, optional choice for restaurant offers. Paying and reviewing never require contact details.
  2. Understand the relationship. Review recorded spend, visit count and last visit. Group first-time customers, regulars and guests who have not returned recently.
  3. Choose an appropriate invitation. Prepare a second-visit message, a regular-guest invitation or a win-back campaign. Your team approves the audience and message before any future sending.
  4. Measure the response. Follow reported deliveries, returning contacts and linked spending. Optional comparison groups help assess results as the audience and observation period become sufficient.

Estimate spending over the next 90 days, identify customers at risk of lapsing and prioritise outreach. Forecasts need sufficient identified visit history, validation and visible uncertainty before they can guide decisions.

An owner dashboard with a clear hierarchy

At a glance: confirmed payment value, identified-bill coverage, marketing opt-in rate and 30-day return rate. Today, 7-day and 30-day views give context, with previous-period comparisons where meaningful.

When needed: completed bills, average bill, payment mix, cash still to collect, tips and campaign results. Deeper customer and service records sit behind the overview.

Agree the scope before the first live bill

  1. Check the fit. Confirm your POS name and version, existing-bill access, payment writeback and merchant payment options, including Whish where supported. Agree how cash recorded in the POS reaches Aalayna; the prototype uses staff confirmation.
  2. Connect and prepare. Set up secure shared records and staff access, test payments and reconciliation, and configure your menu and tables. Connect restaurant-owned messaging accounts and opt-out handling before sending receipts or offers.
  3. Review the evidence. Compare guest usage, settlement time, staff effort, reconciliation, contact capture and marketing permission. Track returning identified guests and campaign outcomes where data and observation time allow.

What we'll ask you

  • How do you contact guests and measure whether they return?
