3alayna is designed for restaurant service in Lebanon: a menu in English, français and عربي, with USD and LBP prices and settlement through supported digital providers or staff-confirmed cash collection.

Three practical outcomes to test

1Easier bill settlement. Guests choose how to split and pay. We measure settlement time and usage during the pilot.
2A simpler staff workflow. Cash requests include the note and change needed. Staff confirm collection; tips are attributed to a server or pool under your policy.
3Clear payment records. Confirmed payments are separate from pending cash, with an export for reconciliation against your POS.
+Your menu and customer connection. Update prices and service hours, offer optional receipt and marketing sign-ups, and give every guest equal access to public reviews and private feedback.

What we’ll ask you

  1. Which POS and version do you use?
  2. How long do guests wait to settle a bill at busy times?
  3. How often do groups ask to split?
  4. Are other parties waiting when tables are settling?
  5. What share of checks settles in cash?
  6. Which payment providers do you already accept?
  7. How are tips distributed and recorded?
  8. Who updates the menu, and how long does it take?
