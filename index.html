How they're connected

  EDITOR ──write──▸ DRAFT
                      │
                   publish  ──▸ menu_versions v47   (immutable)
                      │              │
                      │         live_version = 47
                      ▼              │
                    TABLE ◂──read────┘   caches by version, never sees a draft
                      │
                   settle  ──▸ settlements[]  ──▸ DASHBOARD
                                                    tonight's statement
                                                    tips owed by server
1 Publishing mints an immutable version. A diner's menu can never change under their thumb mid-meal, and rolling back is just pointing live_version at the one before.
2 An open check is priced at order time. Reprice the menu +8% and tonight's bills do not move. Retroactive repricing would be a serious bug, not a feature.
3 Settlement only moves forward on a provider callback or an operator action. A diner returning to the page is not confirmation — they can fake a redirect, not a webhook.
4 Cash-declared is a claim, not a collection. It shows on the statement as declared and stays there until someone counts the drawer.
5 Translations are keyed by dish id, never by position. Adding a dish in the editor used to shift every Arabic name onto the wrong plate.
localStorage stands in for the backend. The apps are separate pages sharing one origin — the same shape they'd have as routes on a real server. Open the editor and the table in two tabs, publish, and watch the menu change.
Payments are simulated. No money moves and no provider is connected.
