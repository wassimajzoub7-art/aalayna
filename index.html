Questions owners ask

The honest answers

What do I need to provide?

Very little. Your menu in any form, even a PDF or photos. Ingredient details per dish so the dietary filters work, which your kitchen confirms in about twenty minutes with our editor. Your Whish or bank merchant account details so payments land with you. Your Google listing. A table count, and one hour with your floor staff for training. No hardware, no POS change.

Do I need to change my POS?

No. 3alayna runs alongside whatever you have. Your POS stays the system of record; our statement is an input to your close, not a replacement for it.

Most of my guests pay cash. Does this still help?

Cash is a first-class flow, not an afterthought. Guests declare they're paying cash, tell the floor which note they're handing over, and your server makes one trip with the right change. The table turns faster even when no money moves digitally.

Where does the money go?

Straight to you. Whish payments go to your Whish account, card payments through your own bank's gateway. 3alayna never holds funds and never sees card numbers.

Will my staff fight it?

Tips are assigned to servers by name, with a payout record. The floor gains from every table that uses the QR.

What do guests need to install?

Nothing. It's a web page that opens from the camera. Works on any phone, in English, French or Arabic, in dollars or lira.
