    Today Yours

      With Aalayna Assumption

        Tables turned at peak Benchmark

        Only counts the services where you turn people away. Minutes saved per table divided by the average sitting, times the tables in play. Platforms abroad report 12 to 15 minutes saved and up to 19% more turnover at peak.

        Tips for your servers Assumption

        On bills settled through Aalayna, a one-tap prompt with 10% preselected. We assume three tables in four tip, at 9% on average. Platforms abroad report tips up two to three times when the prompt appears at payment.

        Google reviews Assumption

        One tap to rate after paying; happy guests are sent to your Google page. We assume 4% of settled tables leave a review. Platforms abroad report five times more reviews.

        Guests you can reach again Assumption

        At the receipt, guests can leave a number or email and separately agree to offers. We assume one in five settled tables opts in, and 8% of a list returns when invited.

        Waiter trips saved Assumption

        Bringing the bill, coming back for the payment, and the walk to the till for change: three trips of about two minutes each on a table that settles itself.

        Cash, declared ahead Yours

        Cash tables tell the floor which note is coming, so change arrives with the receipt. Same cash, one trip instead of two, and no "we'll bring it right back".

        How the pilot proves or disproves this

        Two months, no fee. From the first service the dashboard records, on your own bills:

        • Share of bills settled through Aalayna, and the median time from bill opened to paid.
        • Digital tips per server, per week.
        • Ratings left and Google reviews per week against your baseline.
        • Receipt and offer sign-ups per paid bill.
        • From month two: how many first-month guests came back within 30 days.

        In week seven we sit down with these numbers and yours from above, side by side. You decide with evidence, not with this page.
