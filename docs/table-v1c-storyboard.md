# Table V1, style C: the storyboard (16 October 2026)

Written before anything was drawn (amendment 6 of `table-brief.md`). One point per screen, one object that carries
it, everything else recedes. Built from style B (paper cut-out, no riding phone).

Rules that hold on every stretch:

- **The stage recedes.** Cloth, runner and chairs are a stage: the chairs drop to a quiet kraft at lower contrast,
  the chairs are drawn at 0.72 strength and every supporting object at 0.62, so they read as further away. Only the hero object is at full strength and full colour.
- **The hero sits on the runner, centred on the stretch**, so on a wide screen the note beside it is centred on the
  same line. A short clay hairline runs from the note toward the table, pointing at the hero.
- **The figure is the second thing the eye sees**, never the first: it sits under the headline in clay, counts up
  once as its stretch arrives, and is smaller in weight than the hero is in mass.
- **Light comes from the top left.** Every paper layer casts its shadow down and to the right by the same small
  depth; objects in the air cast a longer, fainter one.
- **Nothing moves while the reader should be reading.** Movement happens in the scroll between notes; when a note
  is centred, its hero is still.
- **Desktop (1280 px and up):** table in the middle, notes alternate sides, each note hugs the table edge. **Phone
  (under 768 px):** the hero drawing, then its note as a paper card lying on the cloth just under it, sized so that
  caption and hero share one 844 px screen. Supporting objects that would crowd a 390 px board are removed.

## Poster (the first screen)

- **Point:** the restaurant's outcome. Nobody waits for the bill, and it costs $150 with two months free.
- **Carrier:** the headline. No drawing.
- **Eye lands on:** "Nobody waits for the bill." **Second:** the price block ($150, the two free months beside it).
- **Recedes:** the name (smaller, above), the three results line (sub colour), "Scan. Split. Settle." (small caps).
- **Motion:** the lines rise in one after another on load, 70 ms apart, 0.7 s each. Nothing loops. The head of the
  table peeks in at the foot of the window as the cue to scroll. **Left out:** any parallax or drawing on the poster.
- **Copy:** name, headline, results line, price and free months, two asks, the three verbs.
- **Desktop:** headline and price form one left-aligned block on a common left edge, the price strip directly under
  the results line. **Phone:** the same stack; the asks sit above the fold at 390 x 844.

## 01 Scan (the QR card)

- **Point:** one code per table, no app.
- **Carrier:** the QR card on the runner.
- **Eye lands on:** the QR card (red finders). **Second:** "Scan." and its line.
- **Recedes:** one place setting at the far side, quiet. The olives of style B are cut.
- **Motion:** the card is set down; a single red scan line passes down the code as it reaches the middle of the
  window. **Left out:** a bounce, a phone pointing at it (a second hero).
- **Caption:** "Scan." / "One code per table. No app to download."
- **Phone:** the setting is dropped; card, then note.

## 02 Browse (the menu on the phone)

- **Point:** the guest reads the menu in their language and currency.
- **Carrier:** the phone, menu on screen, the EN / FR / AR and $ / LL switches at the top.
- **Eye lands on:** the phone's screen. **Second:** "Browse."
- **Recedes:** the guest's plate and cutlery, quiet, at their place. The bread basket is cut.
- **Motion:** the menu's head, then the dishes, come up one after another as the phone is set down.
  **Left out:** scrolling inside the phone.
- **Caption:** "Browse." / "In their language. In dollars or lira."

## Dinner ("Sahtein"): cut

It had no point of its own, it put eight objects on the table and it doubled the distance between the menu and the
bill. The reader knows dinner happens. The bill stretch already says it is over (one used plate pushed aside). The
breath it gave is kept as air: generous space between stretches.

## 03 The bill (the receipt on a brass tray)

- **Point:** the bill reaches the guest's phone the moment they ask; 10 minutes saved per table.
- **Carrier:** the receipt, on a brass tray. The receipt is the thing that will travel.
- **Eye lands on:** the receipt's total. **Second:** "10 min".
- **Recedes:** an arak carafe and a glass to one side, one used plate pushed aside. Four used plates of style B cut.
- **Motion:** the tray is set down. Then, over at least half a screen, the receipt lifts off the tray (rotation,
  shadow lengthening), travels down the table and lands in the split phone, where the screen takes it.
  **Left out:** anything else moving at the same time.
- **Caption:** "The bill." / "On their phone the moment they ask." / 10 min.

## 04 Split

- **Point:** split any way; four friends, $38.13 each.
- **Carrier:** the phone: the bill's head, then ÷4, then four shares.
- **Eye lands on:** the four equal shares. **Second:** "Split."
- **Recedes:** a coffee cup, quiet.
- **Motion:** ÷4 drops in, the four shares come up one after another, 7vh apart. **Left out:** counting digits.
- **Caption:** "Split." / "Evenly, by dish or by amount." / Book a demo (placement "table").

## 05 Settle (the payment)

- **Point:** they pay their share by Whish, card or cash, and the tip is suggested: 22%.
- **Carrier:** the phone: your share, the tip, three ways, Pay.
- **Eye lands on:** the red Pay button and the share. **Second:** "22%".
- **Recedes:** nothing. The bank card and cash note of style B are cut: the screen already says Whish, card, cash.
- **Motion:** three shares leave for the other guests while yours travels on, grows and becomes the payment's first
  line; then the tip, the ways and the button come up in turn. **Left out:** a confirmation tick (it would end the
  stretch before the figure is read).
- **Caption:** "Settle." / "Whish, card or cash." / 22%.

## 06 Rate (the review card)

- **Point:** every table is asked; 83% of those asked leave a review.
- **Carrier:** the review card; its five stars.
- **Eye lands on:** the stars. **Second:** "83%".
- **Recedes:** nothing else on the board but the chairs.
- **Motion:** the five stars arrive one by one, 7vh apart, from small to full. **Left out:** a sparkle.
- **Caption:** "Rate." / "One tap after paying, then Google." / 83%.

## 07 Again (the regulars)

- **Point:** you know who comes back. 50% of orders can come from the few who return.
- **Carrier:** a new object, the regulars card: three names with their visit count, newest first.
  Style B's olive branch said "the table is set again" but not "you know who they are".
- **Eye lands on:** the card's names and counts. **Second:** "50%".
- **Recedes:** two fresh settings, quiet, at the far edges.
- **Motion:** the three rows come up one after another as the card is set down. **Left out:** a chart.
- **Caption:** "Again." / "Guests opt in. You see who comes back." / 50% / Book a demo (placement "outcomes").

## For your restaurant (the price)

- **Point:** $150 a month a location, the first two months at $0.
- **Carrier:** the restaurant's own bill: Month 1 $0, Month 2 $0, Month 3 $150.
- **Eye lands on:** the two olive $0. **Second:** the $150 headline.
- **Recedes:** nothing on the board but the chairs.
- **Motion:** the receipt is set down, its three rows come up in turn. **Left out:** a stamp.
- **Caption:** $150 / month / location, the founding rate, the free pilot, Book a demo (placement "pricing").

## The close (Reserved)

- **Point:** your table is ready; book a demo.
- **Carrier:** a folded "Reserved" card at the foot of the table, then the ask under it.
- **Eye lands on:** "Your table is ready." **Second:** the red Book a demo.
- **Motion:** the card is set down as the foot arrives; nothing else. The runner, which has unfolded ahead of the
  reader all the way down, ends at the card.
- **Caption:** "Your table is ready." / "Tell us about your restaurant. The rest is on us."

## The thread

Revised after the founder saw style B live: the runner no longer unfolds (a moving cloth distracts). The stage is
static; the continuous thread is the chain of hero objects (receipt, share, Paid), set out in
`table-v1c-motion.md`.
