What these numbers mean

Collected payments exclude tips, pending cash and payments now refunded. Contact capture counts bills with an identified payer, not every diner. Marketing sign-ups use each contact’s last receipt choice in the period; repeated submissions count once.

Return visits cover known payers and their first observed visit, not every customer or necessarily their first-ever visit. Comparisons use the previous equal-length period; a dash means there is no eligible data. Historical figures reflect current payment and refund status.
